Product URL: https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/intrinsically-safe-thermometers/921b-intrinsically-safe-thermocouple-thermometer/

Complete Product Description:
The Model 921B is a single-channel intrinsically safe digital thermometer compatible with type K, J, T,E , N, R, S  or B thermocouple probes to provide a wide range of temperature measurements, (-418 °F to 2502 °F) certified intrinsically safe for use in potentially hazardous environments. The 921B boasts a 2000 hour battery life using three AA batteries.  The 921B provides dependable and uninterrupted portable measurement capability without expensive batteries, AC adapters or chargers.
